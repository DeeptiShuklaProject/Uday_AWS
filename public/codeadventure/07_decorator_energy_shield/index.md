# Level 7: Decorators & Function Buffs 🛡️✨

Welcome to **CodeAdventure Level 7**! 🎮

In this level, you will master **Decorators** and **Higher-Order Functions**!

### 💡 Core Concepts Taught:
- **First-Class Functions**: Passing functions as arguments and returning functions from functions.
- **Decorators (`@decorator`)**: Wrapping existing functions to add power-ups (logging, buffing, defense) without modifying their original code!
- **Middleware Pattern**: Intercepting calls to modify input parameters (e.g. doubling damage `damage * 2`) and wrapping output strings with plasma shield banners!

---

### 🎮 Mission Objective:
Define a `@power_buff` decorator function that doubles laser attack damage and activates energy shield aura before firing at enemy dreadnoughts!
